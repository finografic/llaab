Arrived After Three Hops

Shortened and syndicated links are the ordinary way articles are shared, so the fetcher must treat a chain of redirects as the expected case rather than an anomaly. What changes across hops is identity: the URL the operator pasted is provenance, and the URL that finally served the document is the basis for canonicalisation and for resolving relative references.

Recording both is what makes an ingested article auditable later. Someone reading the stored node six months on can see where the link came from and where it actually went, without having to re-follow a chain that may no longer exist.

The budget matters as much as the bookkeeping. A fixed maximum number of hops turns an unbounded loop into a typed failure, and revalidating each target keeps the chain from being used to reach somewhere the first check would have refused.
